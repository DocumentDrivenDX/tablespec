# Voice

**Authority:** [`docs/helix/01-frame/brand-voice.md`](docs/helix/01-frame/brand-voice.md)

That document is the single source of product language for tablespec: audience,
principles, approved/avoided phrases, page rules, and how to extend the voice.

Do not maintain a second full voice guide. Edit `brand-voice.md` when rules
change; keep this file as a pointer only.

## Quick spine

tablespec defines the source-semantic ingested bronze contract and compiles one
UMF into the SQL, dbt, Lakeflow, schema, and validation artifacts a data
platform actually runs.

## When writing

1. Open `docs/helix/01-frame/brand-voice.md`.
2. Prefer contract, artifacts, and commands over process or tracker meta.
3. After a repeated bad pattern, add a **Say This, Not That** row there.
